*Connected Environment: is it safe to work right now?*

Connected Environment answers first: the verdict, the steps to take, then the numbers behind it. One area per project, inside the portal. The 56-second demo below was recorded read-only on live sites.

*What the demo shows*
• *Weather Station, verdict first.* A verdict strip leads the page with the steps to take. An offline station is named and flagged, and readiness shows Not ready.
• *Lightning, status and alarm history.* Each device shows its status, its alert zones and how long the state has held. The alarm history lists every yellow, orange or red interval, with CSV export.
• *Reports and the Safety Policy.* Maximum heat index and wind speed for any period, with a daily breakdown. In Settings, the stop-work limits show what they would have done over the last 30 days, and the policy has a Change history.

*Plus*
• All times show in Saudi time.
• Copy as image on the readings board, to share it in a chat.
• Exports: Excel from Reports, CSV from the alarm history.
• Settings › Connected Products: an Active or Inactive switch per product, per project.
• Heat-index bands: five bands, each with its own work, rest and water settings.

*Where to find it*
Project › Connected Environment (left rail) › Weather Station, Lightning, Reports or Settings.
