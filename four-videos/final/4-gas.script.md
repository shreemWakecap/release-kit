## Left out on purpose (and why)

- The Detector ID of the expanded row (g-detector-detail-c): the frame is not used, because it shows a record ID and its message duplicates the Detectors row ("No current reading. Last known values, not current:").
- Who closed the three Closed alerts, and when: the page does not say; we never describe it.
- The SOS and tipped-over rows: not interpreted. The Alerts beats say only what the page shows (Closed rows have no button, acknowledged rows have one).
- "Critical open 0" tile: on screen in g10, g11 (dimmed, right edge) and g12, never narrated or labelled (it sits beside rows labelled Critical and could be misread either way).
- "Peak today", "Longest quiet gap": Not available yet on the dashboard, not narrated.
- Exposure, exceedances, compliance percentage, TWA and STEL values: not narrated (Not available yet on the page).
- Gas notifications, Observation Manager, CO and CO2, the Zones tab, any SAFE hero, "acknowledge back to the vendor": not claimed, not shown.
- Green SAFE chips: page content on the Detectors table (g09) and the Compliance table (g14), visible at the settled camera. They are never narrated or called out; "safe" is spoken only inside the page quote "Not confirmed safe". The earlier crop that hid them cut the Exposure card title and the gas names, so it was dropped.
- "Trends, Planned" in the left menu: on screen only, never mentioned.
- Gas on the mesh or Modbus: not claimed (a Modbus path for gas was cancelled).
- g-mobile-desktopview-c: duplicate of the phone view at desktop width, not used.
- g-detector-detail-c: not used (see above).

## Assumptions

1. "Live alarms counts alerts nobody has acknowledged; Acknowledged counts those someone has but not yet closed; Open alerts counts alerts not yet closed" is taken from the refresh note and the tile text ("None waiting for a person", "Live count from the Summary read"). With Open 12 and Acknowledged 12, the narration says all twelve are acknowledged, which is why Live alarms reads zero. It never says "being handled" and never says who handles them.
2. "Acknowledged: still open" rests on the Overview tile ("Open, being handled") and the Alerts tiles (Open 12, Acknowledged 12, Closed has no count).
3. The Close button (and the Show detail click on the offline row in g09) is a demonstration (click callout): "this is where you close it", "with edit access" (changes#gas-ack-close, live yes). Nothing was closed by us. The three Closed rows are described only as visible.
4. g12 quotes the page note in its own words ("It is not sent to Blackline: an alert closed here stays open in Blackline Live") and glosses once that Blackline is the gas vendor; the umbrella uses the same sentence. The lead-in says only that closing is recorded here; no who or when is claimed. The caption says "Recorded in WakeCap. Not sent to Blackline."
5. Cadence: no polling or reporting numbers are spoken or drawn (not live-verified). Narration says WakeCap asks the vendor cloud on a timer and each detector reports on its own schedule; the screen shows how old each reading is. The lane label is "Vendor cloud" (Gas is never on the mesh and never Modbus).
6. Staleness: g16 says only what is on screen. The Overview tile "Detectors reporting" counts readings newer than 60 min (the number is not spoken), and an offline detector reads Check and shows its last reading time (the fifth). The old claim that a detector quiet for over an hour reads Check is removed. The diagram sink reads "Reading age. Offline reads Check".
7. "WakeCap store: readings and alerts" in the diagram: G07 (live yes). Gas does not use the mesh or Modbus: G12 (live no); the diagram shows "Vendor cloud" as the lane label and first step, and the g15 caption says "through the vendor's cloud only". Step texts: "on a regular timer", "each reading shows its age".
8. Oxygen: narration says the card shows a low limit of nineteen point five and the page says its low alarm is not raised yet. It never says every gas is checked against its limits.
9. Limits are read from the frames only (H2S alarm above 10 ppm, LEL alarm above 20 %LEL). The page offers no control to change them in these frames; nothing is said about editing.
10. Chapter bar on shots prints the fixed tool prefix "OPEN" before the path, in one style: "Connected Environment › Gas › <page>" (Dashboard is the menu item; the page heading is Overview, said once in g03: "The Gas dashboard opens on Overview", then Overview everywhere). It is a location path, not an instruction on read-only beats.
11. Live values spoken: five detectors, four online, one offline (on screen and part of the point), and twelve (Open alerts and Acknowledged tiles). No reading ages, serials or times are read aloud.
12. g11 narration says "Live alarms on the Overview" because the Alerts page has no Live alarms tile. Open 12 and Acknowledged 12 (Overview tile) tie to Live alarms 0.
13. Chapter 2 "Wallboard and phone", path "By address", covers g07 and g08 (same treatment and wording as the Lightning video): the views have no menu link, and g07 says they open by web address, not from the menu. Chapters 3 to 5 are Detectors, Alerts, Compliance.
