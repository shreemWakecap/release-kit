## DIRECTION beats

None. Every beat describes something visible in a captured frame or a live_today yes claim. No AI agent, gas, notifications or Observation Manager. No beat carries the DIRECTION badge.

## Left out on purpose (and why)

- "Held for": never narrated, called out or zoomed. It can show for about one second when a scene starts on the full frame (l02, l06); every held zoom is framed clear of it (decision 2).
- The Settings intro line ("These are the radii the alerting service uses..."): now BLURRED in the frame by the lead, so no zoom workaround remains; never read aloud. The l12 camera frames the menu (Settings, Lightning) and l13 frames the whole radii card.
- The note "Set manually until Management Maps supports lightning sensor locations.": blurred in the frame, never read aloud; the narration says only "entered by hand, marked Temporary" (the Temporary badge stays visible).
- Claims that the radii change what the alarm does, and that the rings show where lightning struck. l13 says only "the distances the tile shows as rings".
- Wallboard and phone view as "new": they are existing views (first Lightning tag v0.0.3), so they are shown as views you open by address and left off the new list.
- Staleness multiples, email or SMS, RED or flashing states, any state other than All Clear, any go-live date, customer or site names.
- The distance numbers: they stay on screen only.
- The unblurred map and any description of the imagery.
- The ORANGE word in the alarm history caption: the l06 and l07 zooms keep it out of view (it can flash by during a glide).
- Detector brand, the Trends tab (Planned), "never lost" wording for the unreadable queue.
- Modbus before it is explained: the lane badge WIRELESS MESH + MODBUS INPUT appears in l10, the same beat that explains Modbus once ("an industrial wiring and data standard many sensors speak"). The weather lane is never called Modbus; Lightning is read through a small input module and shows up on the mesh as a Modbus device (L01).
- Frames l-mobile-b and l-mobile-hist-b: they add nothing beyond l-mobile-phone-b (history is a folded row).
- Vendor words, Gas, Weather Station and the AI agent: other videos.

## Assumptions

1. Wallboard and phone view: "open by address, a web address, not from the menu" (lead decision E) from the capture (navigated by URL) and changes#lt-wallboard-mobile. Chapter 3 is "Wallboard and phone", path text "By address". The phone tile is a 430 px composite, so the video says "phone width view". The phone callout rects were measured by eye on the frame because the phone rects in captures.json sit about 15 px off.
2. "WakeCap treats only the green state as safe to work" rests on integration-and-devices#L05 (a rule, not page text). All Clear is described as what the page showed when captured; the video never says a site is safe. The caption carries the same attribution as the narration.
3. "Its own permission, apart from viewing" rests on changes#settings-area and integration-and-devices#N03, #X02. The grant name is never shown.
4. Export CSV is only called greyed out; nothing about when it becomes active. Captions keep "in this window" for the alarm numbers.
5. Edit alerting radii and Set location are named as buttons only; nothing claimed to work (L11 live_today unknown) or to change the alarm (L10).
6. Saudi time: pages print "AST, UTC+3"; narration says Saudi time.
7. Click callouts (Settings, Lightning) are demonstrations; nothing was clicked in the capture.
8. The history has many short Data unavailable rows even at All Clear; the narration says the page shows the gaps and never says the data is continuous.
9. The satellite map is blurred so heavily that only a faint blob shows; the narration points at the legend text, the caption says "Map blurred". Red and Yellow radii are called "alert distances" in narration (lead decision H); on-screen page labels keep the page word radii.
10. Modbus is explained once, in l10, in the approved wording. The queue wording is the design (L02 caveat: queue existence not verified live): captions and narration say "set aside", never "never lost"; the diagram step is labelled Cloud queue per the brief.
11. "New" is used for the radii map, alarm chart and history, and Lightning settings, as named by the brief and changes.json live_in_1_0_5 yes. The wallboard and phone view is not called new.
12. The close tells the viewer, with the entitlement qualifier, "if your project has Lightning, open it and read the banner at the top"; the card heading is "Read the banner" and its sub-line says "If your project has Lightning, it is in the menu" (X05, X02 are necessary conditions; the front end also needs a lightning device on the project).
13. The history title "Alarm history" is out of shot in the l07 zoom (kept so the ORANGE caption word stays out and Export CSV stays in); the empty-state line and Export CSV carry the beat.

14. Chapter paths follow one style: "Connected Environment › <page> › <sub page>" (chapters 1, 2 and 4); chapter 3 prints "By address". The builder now prints OPEN before the path. In narration the in-app list is "the menu".
15. "New" follows the brief and changes.json (radii map, alarm chart and history, Lightning settings); the baseline for the customer audience is for the lead to confirm. The wallboard and phone view stays off the new list.
