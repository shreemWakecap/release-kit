# Connected Environment setup tour: narration script

Screen recording of the live portal. No product was switched on or off, and no policy, limit or setting was saved or published. The only writes were to the recorder's own featured-card layout (Select parameters and the card buttons), put back as it was afterwards.

Voice: Gemini TTS (Aoede). Times are where each line starts in the final cut.

## 1 · Explore the Weather Station page

**0:00** · caption: Weather Station: explore, enable, configure
> This is the Weather Station page for one project. First we explore it. Then we see how products are enabled, and how each one is configured.

**0:11** · caption: The answer: Danger, with recommended steps
> The red strip is the answer: Danger, in words. Click View Recommended Steps to open the actions behind it.

**0:21** · caption: Actions in priority order
> They come in priority order, and the first one is marked Do first.

**0:26** · caption: Details: verdict, data confidence, last update
> Details opens the full verdict, with the data confidence and the time of the last update.

**0:33** · caption: Featured cards: heat index, work, rest and water
> Below, the featured cards. The heat index card shows its band, and the work, rest and water cycle for the crew.

**0:42** · caption: Copy as image and Export (not clicked)
> Copy as image copies your selected cards as a picture, to paste and share. And Export, at the top right, exports the weather data.

**0:51** · caption: Stations: click one to see its panel
> On the right are the stations, each Online or Offline. Click a station to view it. A panel shows its identifier and the time of its last reading.

**1:01** · caption: The pencil renames a station (not clicked)
> The pencil next to a station renames it.

**1:05** · caption: An offline station: no readings, said plainly
> Click the offline station and the page says it plainly: no readings for the selected range, and the station appears to be offline. Click an online station to come back.

## 2 · Choose what to feature

**1:17** · caption: Gear: Select parameters, one switch per reading
> The gear opens Select parameters. Every reading is a switch: temperature, wind, humidity, rainfall, pressure, dust, particles and gases. Switch one off, and it leaves the featured area.

**1:34** · caption: Remove a card from the featured area
> A card also has its own small button. Use it on barometric pressure, and the card leaves the list.

**1:42** · caption: Switch it on again: the card returns at the end
> Back in Select parameters, switch it on again, and the card returns, at the end of the list.

**1:52** · caption: Move a card up or down, or remove it
> Each card can also move up, move down, or leave the featured area.

**1:58** · caption: A reading above its limit turns red
> A reading above its limit turns red and says Above safe limit.

## 3 · Reports

**2:04** · caption: Reports: pick a period
> Reports holds the Maximum Values Report. Pick a period: today, yesterday, this week, the last seven days, this month, the last thirty days, or a custom range.

**2:17** · caption: Peak heat index, peak wind, daily table, Excel export
> You get the peak heat index and the peak wind speed, a daily table, and an Excel export.

**2:38** · caption: Switch on: the product joins the menu (not switched here)

**2:46** · caption: A project with Lightning on: menu entry
> Here is a project where Lightning is on. The menu has a Lightning entry.

**2:52** · caption: A project with Gas on: menu entry and status
> And one where Gas is on. The menu shows Gas, with its status.

## 5 · Configure Weather Station

**2:58** · caption: Settings, Weather Station: the Site Safety Policy
> Settings, Weather Station holds the Site Safety Policy. The page says it plainly: changing anything here changes when work stops, for everyone on the project.

**3:10** · caption: Right now: current band and the cycle in force
> At the top, Right now shows the current band, and the work, rest and water cycle in force on site.

**3:18** · caption: Limits: plus and minus, count switch, 30 day chart
> Each reading has a limit, set with plus and minus, and a switch to count it in the policy. For temperature and wind, a thirty day chart shows what the limit would have done.

**3:30** · caption: Heat index bands: work, rest and water
> Below, the heat index bands. Each band has its own work, rest and water cycle.

**3:38** · caption: Tabs: calculation method and change history
> Two more tabs: the heat index calculation method, and the change history.

## 6 · Configure Lightning and Gas

**3:46** · caption: Lightning settings without a device
> Lightning has its own settings. In this project no lightning device is provisioned yet, and the page says so.

**3:53** · caption: Edit alerting radii: red, yellow, all-clear delay
> Where a device reports, the page shows its alert distances and its sensor location. Edit alerting radii opens a small form: the red distance, the yellow distance, and the all-clear delay.

**4:07** · caption: Set location: click the map or type coordinates
> Set location lets you click the map or type coordinates. The location is marked Temporary.

**4:15** · caption: Gas: no settings page, limits are read only
> Gas has no settings page. Its limits are shown, read only, on the Compliance page.
