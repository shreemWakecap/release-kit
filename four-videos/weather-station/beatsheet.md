## Left out on purpose (and why)
- Station display names: blurred in the frames, never narrated (lead decision 1).
- The green 'Last reading was N minute(s) ago.' line under the offline headline: no zoom or spotlight contains it. It is visible, small and un-narrated, for under a second while the camera glides out from the full frame at the start of each scene on the dashboard frame (w02 and w06); a sliver can show during the glide into w07 (lead decision 4: short glides accepted). On the second project (w08) the zoom now starts below the same line (it sits under an ONLINE station), so it shows only in the half-second entry glide.
- Second project readiness (Ready): no longer narrated or zoomed, because the only zoom that holds the Ready chip also holds the green last-reading line (brief Avoid list). The beat now reads only the three readings in Danger.
- 'Held for', Lightning and Gas content: not in this video. The second project's Gas strip and rail row show for the half-second entry glide of w08 only and are not narrated.
- Heat band numbers: on screen in the policy cards (they sit beside the band colour dots, which the narration points at), never narrated, captioned, labelled or spotlighted; callout labels carry no numbers (lead decision 3). The ribbon frame (w-policy-a-bands) is unused: its labels carry the numbers and the 50.1 marker.
- Heat index values 49.5 (dashboard) and 50.1 (policy page) differ between captures (page clocks 3:23 PM and 3:25 PM): neither is narrated; 50.1 stays visible, dimmed, in the w11 zoom because the banner cannot be cropped out at that scale.
- Why the station is offline, or whether it affects the verdict, frozen or stale sensors, what the data confidence bars mean: the page does not say, so the video does not. The hook sub line therefore says "the data behind it", not "how far to trust it".
- How readiness is decided: no research claim defines it; the video only reads what the page shows.
- Policy edit, stage and publish path: never exercised, not claimed. Only the page's own warning is read, and the caption says 'new home' because the policy itself is older than its redesigned page.
- Gear popup: the narration says "switches choose which readings are featured", not "one switch per reading", because Heat Index has no switch (pinned). CO2, H2S and CO switches are the page's own weather-parameter list, not narrated. The caption marks the popup as new, not the choosing.
- Reports Last 30 Days frame (w-reports-a), w-menu-a, w-policy-a-method, w-top-c: unused (duplicates or length). The method tab is shown through the NOAA row in w-policy-a-cards.
- Station rename, Copy as image, Copy actions, Export: never clicked; only 'you can' wording. The Export callout is a plain pointer (no cursor animation), because the Excel download was not exercised in capture.
- Change history sentence "Older changes may sit further back": dropped from the narration to hold the length ceiling; it stays on screen under a spotlight.
- Agent or AI features, other products' paths (Lightning ADAM, gas vendor cloud): umbrella video only.
