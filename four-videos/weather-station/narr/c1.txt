On site, a supervisor reads a heat number and asks: so what now? A number cannot say. An answer can.

Weather Station answers in one strip. Danger, in words, and an instruction: act on the recommended steps.

Need the actions? You can click View Recommended Steps, right in the strip, to open them.

Two actions, in priority order. First, pause outdoor work and move crews to shade or rest.

Then Details. It shows the verdict, a data confidence mark, and the last update, in Saudi time.

The heat card also carries the cycle in force. Thirty minutes of work, ten of rest, water every fifteen.
