Is it safe to work right now? Heat says one thing. Lightning says another. Gas says a third. You need one place to look.

That place is Connected Environment. One umbrella over weather, lightning and gas, in the portal.

Under it sit Weather Station, Lightning and Gas, each with its own screens and devices.

They share one platform: one backend, one menu and header, the same view permission. Projects switch on what they use.

Your site team watches and decides. Control is narrower. Weather limits decide when work stops. A Gas alert can be acknowledged or closed, recorded in WakeCap only.

Connected Products lists what each project has switched on. Weather alone, Lightning alone, Weather with Gas.
