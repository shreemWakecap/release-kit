## Assumptions

- Chips: one backend, one menu and header, same view permission, switched on per project (X01, X02, X05; shared header in u-ws-c). No 'one rule set', 'one audit trail', 'one permission for everything'.
- 'Control' is scoped in u04 as the lead decided: Weather Station limits that decide when work stops (k01 shows the 30 day what-if preview) and Gas alerts that can be acknowledged or closed, recorded in WakeCap only (k03, k03b). Lightning is not under Control. The team card still shows the verb 'Control' and the builder draws its connector to the Weather card only (reported as a builder limitation).
- No blanket 'never falsely safe': c02 (weather offline named, Not ready), g01 (Check and Not confirmed safe, on the phone page) and l01 (only green is safe, a rule of the product) each say only what is shown or what the rule is.
- Captions say 'Recently added' for Connected Products only, where changes.json says live_in_1_0_5 yes and a frame shows it; the narration makes no date claim.
- Weather and Lightning devices both reach WakeCap over a wireless mesh through a gateway; the captured devices are in different projects, so one shared physical network is not claimed.
- Project A, B and C labels are frame suffixes, not names; three different projects with different products on. The video never presents them as one site.
- Time zone: no clock time or date is narrated, so the Saudi time question does not arise.
- Chapter paths use one style, 'Connected Environment > page > sub page'; the builder prints OPEN before them. Chapter 2 is a cross-product overview and lists its destinations. Only the callouts with click:true (k03b Close, z01 rail icon) are demonstrations; nothing was clicked in capture.
