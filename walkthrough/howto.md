# How to explore Connected Environment

A written version of the walkthrough video. Six live pages, in the order of the video. Everything here was read from live sites in read-only mode. Times on every page are Saudi time (AST, UTC+3).

## Before you start

1. Open the portal and pick a project with the project picker, top right.
2. In the left rail, click the sun-and-cloud icon. Its tooltip says **Connected Environment**.
3. The menu beside it lists what the project has: **Weather Station**, **Reports**, **Settings**, and **Lightning** where Lightning is switched on. **Trends** is marked Planned and is not available yet.

This tour never presses Edit, Save or the Active/Inactive switch. Do the same unless you mean to change the site.

---

## 1. Weather Station

**Where to click:** Connected Environment › Weather Station

**What you are looking at:** a verdict strip across the top, the Weather parameters board, and a station health panel on the right.

**How to read it**
- **Verdict strip.** Read the colour first. On a bad day it is red and says Danger. Next to it are **Data confidence** and the **last update time**. Check that time. Old data can look fine.
- **Heat Index card.** It takes the colour of its band and shows the **work time**, **rest time** and **water** to follow. **Data as of** says when the readings were taken. **Copy as image** copies the board so you can paste it into a chat.
- **Station health.** Every station is listed as Online or Offline. The panel says whether the site is **Ready** or **Not ready**, and what needs attention. An offline station is named, not hidden.
- **View Recommended Steps.** The actions are listed in priority order, with **Do first** on the top one. **Details** shows the full verdict, the data confidence and the freshness of the data.

## 2. Reports

**Where to click:** Connected Environment › Reports

**What you are looking at:** the Maximum Values Report.

**How to read it**
- Pick a **period** at the top: Today, Yesterday, This Week, Last 7 Days, This Month, Last 30 Days, or Custom.
- Two tiles show the **maximum heat index** and the **maximum wind speed** for that period, when each happened and at which station. The heat index carries its band.
- The **daily breakdown** lists the highest values for every day across all stations. The coloured dot beside a heat index is its band.
- **Export .xlsx** saves the report as an Excel file.

## 3. Settings › Connected Products

**Where to click:** Connected Environment › Settings › Connected Products

**What you are looking at:** a list of the products on the project, each with a switch.

**How to read it**
- **Active** means the product is switched on for this project and appears in the menu.
- **Inactive** means it is switched off and does not appear in the menu.
- Do not touch the switch unless you mean to turn a product on or off for the whole project.

## 4. Settings › Weather Station (Site Safety Policy)

**Where to click:** Connected Environment › Settings › Weather Station

**What you are looking at:** the limits and cycles that decide when work stops on this site. The page warns that changing anything here changes when work stops, for everyone on the project.

**How to read it**
- **Right now** shows the current band and the cycle in force: minutes of work, minutes of rest, and the water to drink.
- **Stop-work limits.** Each sensor has a limit. Work stops when a reading reaches it. For temperature and wind speed, a chart shows the last 30 days, and the red bars are days the limit would have stopped work.
- **Heat index bands.** The ribbon runs from Normal to Extreme Danger, and a line marks where the heat index is now. A strip counts the last 30 days by band.
- **Band cards.** Each band card sets the work time, rest time and water for that band. The band the heat index is in is the guidance you see on the Weather Station page.
- **Change history** (tab at the bottom). It lists changes to the policy. On the project checked it said no changes were recorded in the history it read.

## 5. Lightning

**Where to click:** Connected Environment › Lightning (on projects where Lightning is switched on)

**What you are looking at:** a state tile for each device, an alarm chart and an alarm history.

**How to read it**
- A banner says WakeCap lightning alerts are a backup. Always follow the site's cabinet lights and sounder first.
- The **tile** shows the state. On the day checked it read **All Clear, Safe to work**.
- The **rings** show the configured alert zones: red within 13 km, yellow 13 to 20 km, with an all-clear setting of 30 minutes. They are a reference, not a live strike position.
- **Held for** says how long the state has lasted, and since when.
- **Alarm activity** and **Alarm history** list every yellow, orange or red interval. On the day checked both were empty, and **Export CSV** was switched off until there is something to export.

## 6. Another project

**Where to click:** the project picker, top right.

Switch to another project. The Weather Station page has the same layout. The panel under the station list names the readings behind the verdict, each with its band or limit. On the day checked, readiness read Ready with one station online.
