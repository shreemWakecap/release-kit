Advocacy brief · pilot stage

Is it safe to work right now?

Connected Environment answers first. Open a project, read the verdict and what to do about it, then check the numbers behind it. Heat and lightning for the site, in one place inside the portal your teams already use.

Answer first.

The page opens on the verdict and the next step, not a wall of numbers.[portal: 2026-10-04 live]

Never falsely safe.

A device that is offline, faulty, stale or unknown is never shown as safe.[spec]

Every change audited.

The site Safety Policy has a Change history on the Settings page.[portal: 2026-10-04 live]

Everything on this page was read from live sites, read-only, on 4 October 2026. All times are Saudi time (AST, UTC+3).[portal: 2026-10-04 live]

1 · The problem on site

Numbers without answers. Data that looks fine and is not.

  • A grid of readings makes the supervisor do the sums. The one question on site is whether work can continue.[spec]
  • A dead or frozen sensor can read as calm. Old data that looks fine is worse than no data.[spec]
2 · What it is

One area per project. Four places to look.

Find it in the portal's left rail, under Connected Environment.[portal: 2026-10-04 live] Access follows the view and edit permissions the project already has.[spec]

Weather Station

Live readings and a heat index with work, rest and drinking-water guidance. The verdict comes first.[portal: 2026-10-04 live]

Where  Connected Environment › Weather Station

Lightning

Status per device with its alert zones, an alarm history and CSV export.[portal: 2026-10-04 live]

Where  Connected Environment › Lightning, where Lightning is switched on

Reports

A Maximum Values Report for the period you pick, with a daily breakdown and an Excel export.[portal: 2026-10-04 live]

Where  Connected Environment › Reports

Settings

Which products are switched on for the project, and the Safety Policy: limits, heat-index bands and a change history.[portal: 2026-10-04 live]

Where  Connected Environment › Settings

4 · Proof, in three tiers

What is promised, what we saw, what a pilot proved.

Targets

  • One verdict leads the page, with what to do next.[spec]
  • An offline, faulty, stale or unknown device never reads as safe.[spec]
  • A safety policy that can face an auditor: in-force values with units, and a history of changes.[spec]

Demo-observed

  • On both Weather Station projects checked, the verdict strip led the page. Both showed Danger at the time.[FE-observation]
  • An offline station was flagged by name, and the panel read Not ready. It was not shown as a calm reading.[FE-observation]
  • Times show in Saudi time. We checked by running the browser in Los Angeles time; the portal still showed Saudi time.[FE-observation]
  • Lightning alarm history and Change history were both empty on the projects checked, so no alarm rows or change entries were seen.[FE-observation]

Pilot-observed

  • None. No pilot results exist yet, and none are claimed on this page.
5 · Rollout and next step

Nothing to install. Start with one project.

  • It opens inside the portal, from the left rail. There is no separate app or login.[portal: 2026-10-04 live]
  • The readings board has a Copy as image button, so a supervisor can share the board into a group chat.[portal: 2026-10-04 live]
  • Hypothesis: a supervisor can read the strip and the steps without training, because the answer comes first. Training time has not been measured.[inferred]
  • Suggested next step, for discussion: pick one project with a Weather Station and let the site HSE lead use it for working shifts, then tell us where the verdict or the steps are unclear.[inferred]
