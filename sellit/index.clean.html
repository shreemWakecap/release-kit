Advocacy brief · pilot stage

Is it safe to work right now?

Connected Environment answers first. Open a project, read the verdict and what to do about it, then check the numbers behind it. Heat and lightning for the site, in one place inside the portal your teams already use.

Answer first.

The page opens on the verdict and the next step, not a wall of numbers.

Never falsely safe.

Every change audited.

The site Safety Policy has a Change history on the Settings page.

Everything on this page was read from live sites, read-only, on 4 October 2026. All times are Saudi time (AST, UTC+3).

1 · The problem on site

Numbers without answers. Data that looks fine and is not.

  • A grid of readings makes the supervisor do the sums. The one question on site is whether work can continue.
  • A dead or frozen sensor can read as calm. Old data that looks fine is worse than no data.
2 · What it is

One area per project. Four places to look.

Find it in the portal's left rail, under Connected Environment. Access follows the view and edit permissions the project already has.

Weather Station

Live readings and a heat index with work, rest and drinking-water guidance. The verdict comes first.

3 · Walkthrough

Six screens, starting with the bad news.

Weather Station, read-only capture, 4 Oct 2026, 12:41 PM AST.

SCREEN 1

The verdict leads the page.

  • Danger sits in a strip across the top, with data confidence and the time of the last update.
  • Heat Index 50.4 °C. In this band: 30 min work, 10 min rest, one cup (250 ml) of water every 15 min.
  • One of three stations is offline. The panel says Not ready and names the station. It does not hide it.

View Recommended Steps (left) and Details (right).

SCREEN 2

What to do, in order.

  • View Recommended Steps lists the actions in priority order: pause outdoor work and move crews to shade or rest, then notify the site safety officer. A Copy actions button sits under the list.
  • Details shows the full verdict, the data confidence and when the data was last updated. Nothing else is claimed.

Lightning, read-only capture, 4 Oct 2026, 12:45 PM AST. One site label is redacted.

SCREEN 3

Lightning: status and alarm history.

  • The device tile reads All Clear, Safe to work. The rings show the configured zones: RED 13 km or less, YELLOW 13 to 20 km, and an ALL-CLEAR setting of 30 min. They are a reference, not a live strike position.
  • The tile says how long the state has held: 114 h 52 m since 29 Sep, 17:53 AST.
  • A banner reminds people that WakeCap lightning alerts are a backup and the site's cabinet lights and sounder come first.
  • Alarm history lists every YELLOW, ORANGE or RED interval in the window. On this project it is empty today, the Alarm activity chart is empty, and Export CSV is disabled.

Reports, last 30 days, 4 Oct 2026, 12:42 PM AST.

SCREEN 4

Reports: the worst day, in one place.

  • Pick a period: Today, Yesterday, This Week, Last 7 Days, This Month, Last 30 Days, or a custom range.
  • Two tiles give the maximums for the period. Last 30 days here: heat index 60.2 °C, Extreme Danger (9 Sep, 3:01 PM) and wind 32.0 km/h (7 Sep, 1:27 PM).
  • A daily breakdown lists the highest heat index and wind speed for every day, across all stations. Export .xlsx is one click away.

Settings: Connected Products and Site Safety Policy. Nothing was edited or saved.

SCREEN 5

Settings: who is on, and what stops work.

  • Connected Products has an Active or Inactive switch for each product on the project. Weather Station is Active here.
  • The Safety Policy page warns that changing anything changes when work stops, for everyone on the project.
  • Each stop-work limit shows what it would have done over the last 30 days: Temperature 46 °C would have stopped work on 8 of 30 days, Wind Speed 32 km/h on 1 of 30.
  • Five heat-index bands (Normal below 25, Caution 25 to 30, Extreme Caution 30 to 39, Danger 39 to 52, Extreme Danger 52 and up) each carry their work, rest and water settings. A 30-day strip shows 18 days in Danger and 12 in Extreme Danger.
  • A Change history tab sits at the bottom. On this project it reads "No changes were recorded in the history we read for this project. Older changes may sit further back than this page reaches."

A second project, read-only capture, 4 Oct 2026, 12:45 PM AST.

SCREEN 6

Same layout on the next project.

  • Danger again, Heat Index 43.3 °C. One station, online, readiness Ready.
  • The panel lists the readings behind the verdict, each with its band or limit: Heat Index in the Danger band, Temperature 41.0 °C and Barometric Pressure 1002.8 hPa past their configured limits.
4 · Proof, in three tiers

What is promised, what we saw, what a pilot proved.

Targets

  • One verdict leads the page, with what to do next.
  • An offline, faulty, stale or unknown device never reads as safe.
  • A safety policy that can face an auditor: in-force values with units, and a history of changes.

Demo-observed

  • On both Weather Station projects checked, the verdict strip led the page. Both showed Danger at the time.
  • An offline station was flagged by name, and the panel read Not ready. It was not shown as a calm reading.
  • Times show in Saudi time. We checked by running the browser in Los Angeles time; the portal still showed Saudi time.
  • Lightning alarm history and Change history were both empty on the projects checked, so no alarm rows or change entries were seen.

Pilot-observed

  • None. No pilot results exist yet, and none are claimed on this page.
5 · Rollout and next step

Nothing to install. Start with one project.

  • It opens inside the portal, from the left rail. There is no separate app or login.
  • The readings board has a Copy as image button, so a supervisor can share the board into a group chat.
  • Hypothesis: a supervisor can read the strip and the steps without training, because the answer comes first. Training time has not been measured.
  • Suggested next step, for discussion: pick one project with a Weather Station and let the site HSE lead use it for working shifts, then tell us where the verdict or the steps are unclear.
