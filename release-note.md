# Connected Environment: what's new

Connected Environment is the safety area for each project in the WakeCap portal. It answers one question first: is it safe to work right now? Weather Station, Lightning, Reports and Settings now sit together in one place.

## What you can do now

- **Weather Station.** Read the verdict first. A strip across the top gives the state and the steps to take. See the Heat Index with its work time, rest time and drinking-water guidance, and check every station, online or offline.
- **Lightning.** Where it is switched on, see each device's state, its alert zones and how long the state has held. Review the alarm history and export it as CSV.
- **Reports.** Pick a period and see the maximum heat index and wind speed, with a daily breakdown you can export to Excel.
- **Settings.** Switch products on or off for a project, and read the Safety Policy: stop-work limits, heat-index bands and a Change history.

## Where to find it

Open your project in the portal. In the left rail, click the sun-and-cloud icon, **Connected Environment**. Then choose **Weather Station**, **Reports**, **Settings** or **Lightning**.

## New since August

- **One area per project.** A side menu you can collapse, and a clock in the header.
- **Reports.** The Maximum Values Report: period buttons, tiles for the maximum heat index and wind speed, a daily breakdown and an Excel export.
- **Safety Policy in Settings.** Each stop-work limit shows what it would have done over the last 30 days. The heat-index bands sit on one ribbon, with work, rest and water settings for each band. A Change history tab lists changes to the policy.
- **Connected Products.** An Active or Inactive switch for each product on a project.
- **Lightning.** A status tile with alert zones and a held-for timer, an alarm activity chart, and an alarm history with CSV export.
- **Weather Station.** Choose which readings to feature with the gear icon. Each station has a Rename button. All times show in Saudi time.

## Good to know

- Times on every page are Saudi time.
- The alarm history and the Change history show a short message when there is nothing to list yet.
- Trends appears in the menu marked Planned. It is not available today.
- The Safety Policy page warns that changing anything changes when work stops, for everyone on the project.
